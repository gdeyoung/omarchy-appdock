APPDOCK / GDEYOUNG

Real minimize & restore for the Omarchy bar.

Omarchy ships with no minimize at all. AppDock puts a task-manager dock in the bar — click the focused app to minimize it, click a dimmed icon to bring it back. Windows park on a hidden workspace, docks are per-workspace, and your keyboard flow never breaks.
Click focused app → minimizes to dock
Native titlebar buttons via hyprbars
Click dimmed icon → restores instantly
Super+Minus / Super+0 for keyboard flow
Per-workspace docks — origins remembered
Middle-click closes; hover for app + title
Dot indicator marks minimized windows
Companions: Window Switcher & Floating Mode
